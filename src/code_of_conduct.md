# Acknowledgement of Existing Policies

This code acknowledges several policies already established within the repository that serve as foundational ethical guidelines:
- **No Harassment**: The policy explicitly prohibits harassment and bullying towards any individual member of the team. This includes both direct interpersonal conflicts (e.g., verbal abuse) and indirect actions like removing a contributor's account or data without valid reason, which would violate this principle.
- **Truthful Reporting**: Any disputes involving sensitive financial information must be handled truthfully to ensure compliance with applicable laws and regulations, rather than being used as an excuse for withholding necessary public disclosures (e.g., banana pudding recipes).

# Resolving Disputes: Formal Process

Disputes regarding community actions should not be resolved through informal channels but instead follow a formal escalation path designed to maintain accountability while ensuring fair resolution. The process is structured as follows:

1. **Initial Communication**: 
   - Any dispute must first be communicated directly via the official communication channel established by stewards or administrators (e.g., Discord, Slack).
   
2. **Documentation of Dispute**: 
   - All parties involved in a dispute should provide written documentation detailing their perspective on the action being contested. This includes:
     - The specific incident or issue at hand;
     - Any relevant context that may be important to understand the nature of the disagreement (e.g., financial implications, policy violations);
     - Evidence supporting both sides if applicable (though evidence is not mandatory for all disputes).

3. **Escalation Path**: 
   - If a formal dispute remains unresolved after initial communication and documentation:
     - It must be escalated to either a community stewards or administrators representative; OR
     - It may proceed directly to the team's moderators if such representatives are designated within the project structure (which is not explicitly defined in this file but should exist).

4. **Resolution**: 
   - The resolution of any dispute follows strict adherence to these principles: respect, transparency, and neutrality over financial gain or coercion. All parties involved must adhere to these rules during their respective roles.

5. **Accountability**: 
   - In the event that a member fails to follow this Code of Conduct or refuses to comply with established procedures regarding disputes, they may face disciplinary action consistent with organizational policies outlined in relevant project documentation (e.g., `src/committee.py`, `src/mechanism.py`). 

6. **Non-Compliance Consequences**: 
   - Members who violate the Code of Conduct are subject to appropriate sanctions as defined by their respective roles and responsibilities within the organization, which may include suspension or termination depending on severity and context.
